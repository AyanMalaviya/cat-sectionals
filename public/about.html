Why We Built CBTMocks (cbtmocks.online)

Every year in India, hundreds of thousands of aspirants prepare for competitive examinations like the Common Admission Test (CAT), XAT, and SNAP. However, access to high-quality mock tests has become increasingly commercialized. Commercial coaching giants offer 1 or 2 free "sample mocks" as marketing bait, then lock their complete test series behind steep ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 paywalls while harvesting phone numbers for aggressive sales calls.

We built CBTMocks to put an end to this paywall exploitation. High-caliber mock test practice should be available to every deserving aspirant, free of cost.
