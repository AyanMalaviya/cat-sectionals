How to Score 99%ile in XAT Decision Making

Decision Making is unique to XAT and determines whether you clear XLRI's sectional cutoff. Most aspirants fail because they treat it as an opinion test. In reality, DM is an exercise in rigorous stakeholder analysis and ethical governance.

1. Multi-Stakeholder Balance
The correct choice rarely maximizes one party's happiness at the expense of fairness. Balance customers, employees, management, and society.
2. Ethical Non-Negotiables
Options that conceal product defects, bribe officials, or shift blame are automatically incorrect regardless of short-term profit.
3. Proactive vs Passive
Avoid options that merely "wait and watch" or "form a committee to delay". Effective leaders take phased, measurable action.
