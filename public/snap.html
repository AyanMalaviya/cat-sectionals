How to Master 60 Questions in 60 Minutes

Unlike CAT, SNAP has no on-screen calculator and no sectional time limits. You can switch sections freely. The secret to a 99+ percentile is strict time capping and eliminating question traps in under 45 seconds.

1. Start with English
Knock out all 15 English questions in 10-12 minutes. You either know the vocabulary or you don't.
2. Rapid Reasoning
Dedicate 25 minutes to Analytical Reasoning. Skip long multi-variable puzzles immediately.

SNAP FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about Free SNAP Mocks

Are these SNAP mocks 100% free?

Yes, completely free. You can practice full-length 60-minute speed tests or individual sectionals with zero signups or payment.

Is there a calculator in the SNAP exam?

No. Unlike CAT, the official SNAP exam does NOT provide an on-screen calculator. All arithmetic and calculations must be performed mentally or on rough scratch sheets.

Are there sectional cutoffs in SNAP?

No, Symbiosis institutes (SIBM Pune, SCMHRD, SIBM Bangalore) evaluate candidates strictly on overall total score. There are no individual sectional cutoffs.
