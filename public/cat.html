CAT Exam Pattern & Sectional Breakdown

Section 1 40 Minutes

Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC)

  • • 24 Questions (16 RC Questions, 8 VA Questions)
  • • 4 Reading Comprehension Passages (~450-500 words each)
  • • Para Jumbles, Para Summary, Odd Sentence Out
  • • Target 99%ile Score: 38-42 marks
Practice VARC Sectionals →
Section 2 40 Minutes

Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR)

  • • 20 Questions (4 sets of 5 questions each)
  • • Matrix grids, Round-robin tournaments, Route networks
  • • Multi-variable grouping, Venn diagrams, Quant-based reasoning
  • • Target 99%ile Score: 30-34 marks (2.5 - 3 full sets)
Practice DILR Sectionals →
Section 3 40 Minutes

Quantitative Aptitude (QA)

  • • 22 Questions (MCQs and TITA non-negative)
  • • Arithmetic (TSD, Work, P&L, Mixtures, SI-CI)
  • • Algebra (Quadratics, Functions, Inequalities, Logs)
  • • Geometry, Modern Math, Number Systems
  • • Target 99%ile Score: 33-37 marks
