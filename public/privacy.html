Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 • Website: https://cbtmocks.online

Summary: CBTMocks is a 100% free open educational portal. We do not require registration, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card information. Your mock test progress is saved directly in your browser's local storage.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not require user signups, email address verifications, phone numbers, or financial information. All 86+ full-length, sectional, and topic-wise mock tests can be taken immediately without creating an account or paying fees.

2. Local Browser Storage (LocalStorage)

To maintain seamless test-taking functionality—including autosaving your answers, question timers, test history, percentiles, and bookmarked questions—our web application utilizes standard HTML5 LocalStorage on your local browser. This data remains on your machine and is never transmitted to or harvested by external tracking databases.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

To maintain server bandwidth, hosting infrastructure, and free mock test delivery for students, CBTMocks displays non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense and its certified partner networks.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Log Files

CBTMocks follows standard hosting practices by using server log files. The information inside log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser user agent, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are strictly used to diagnose technical errors and safeguard site security.

5. Children's Information (COPPA)

Protecting the privacy of young students is especially important. CBTMocks is an educational website intended for candidates preparing for competitive post-secondary examinations. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.
