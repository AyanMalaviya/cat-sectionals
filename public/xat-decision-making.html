The 5-Step XAT Decision Making Elimination Framework

Unlike standard reading comprehension, Decision Making tests your executive judgment as a future corporate leader.

1 The Non-Negotiable Ethics Rule

Any option that involves bribing, misrepresenting facts to customers or regulators, covering up defects, or breaking the law is instantly wrong. XLRI places corporate governance above all else.

2 Multi-Stakeholder Optimization

Good managers don't choose "either/or" when a compromise exists. Options that benefit one stakeholder while devastating another (e.g. firing loyal workers to boost quarterly EBITDA) are rarely the key.

3 Reject the "Passive Delay" Trap

Options that say "wait and watch", "appoint a third-party committee to investigate over 6 months", or "defer the decision until next year" are almost always distractors designed to catch indecisive students.

4 Proportionate & Measurable Action

Responses should match the scale of the problem. A single junior employee's tardiness does not warrant a company-wide disciplinary overhaul. Focus on surgical, actionable solutions.
